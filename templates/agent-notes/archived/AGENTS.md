# AGENTS.md: archived Agent Notes

Archived notes under the class folders are frozen historical snapshots, not current authority. Never edit, reformat, translate, repair, move, or delete a sealed note; record new decisions and facts in an active note or current documentation.

An archival change may only move a complete note (with any translated counterpart) from `implemented/<class>/` to `archived/<class>/`, insert an identical `Archived: YYYY-MM-DD` line below each `Status: implemented` line, and repair or delete inbound links from active prose. Do not inspect, verify, or repair links that point out of archived notes. Follow the [`agent-notes`](../../skills/agent-notes/SKILL.md) workflow.
